## Part A: the four price guides

**No traffic data exists for any of them.** Similarweb's free page shows no figures, and none of the four domains appeared in about 18 Google results for "cuánto cuesta una página web en Perú" or "precio página web Perú costo diseño web Lima". Those results are taken by kom.pe (around 15 URLs), GoDaddy LATAM, neolo.com, tiendanube, crehana and websiteplanet. Expect little referral traffic from any of these sites. A link has some value; a mention in a cited source has more, because AI engines read these guides.

Three of the four guides (terrenoweb, publicita, limafocus) were published or updated in October 2026 under very similar URL patterns, with no traffic. They may be new or auto-generated sites. Treat any link from them as cheap, not as authority.
