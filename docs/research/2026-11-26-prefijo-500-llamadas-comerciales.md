# Peru "500" prefix for commercial calls and SMS (research notes, 2026-11-26)

Method: web search plus page fetches on 2026-11-26. The El Peruano HTML text of the decree was read directly (via the fetch tool summary). The PDF could not be text-extracted. Anything marked UNVERIFIED was not confirmed in a source.

## Verdict on the premise
The premise is CORRECT, with two nuances: (a) the prefix is 500, a non-geographic series, format `500 XXX XXX`; (b) the issuing body is the MTC (not OSIPTEL, not Indecopi). The deadline for operators is about 15 March 2027. It is NOT a start date for companies calling. See "Open points".

## 2. What DS 018-2026-MTC requires (El Peruano text, 15 Sep 2026)
- Norma: 4 chapters, 15 articles, 6 final complementary provisions, 1 modifying provision, 4 annexes.
- Art. 5: the non-geographic series "500" is reserved EXCLUSIVELY for originating commercial/advertising calls and SMS. Format `500 XXX XXX` (9 digits). Fixed and mobile (art. 11).
- Art. 10: operators may assign 500 numbers only to providers that send commercial/advertising information exclusively; "queda prohibida la asignacion de cualquier otra numeracion para dichos fines". One number cannot be assigned to two providers at once. Portability applies.
- Art. 11: operators ensure identification, routing and traceability at all interconnection points; the full number must be visible to the recipient.
- Art. 7-9: initial assignment up to 5 blocks of 1,000 numbers; more blocks require 70% usage; blocks unused for 12 months can be reverted.
- Art. 8: monthly report to MTC (first 5 business days) on assignments per provider; semiannual usage reports.
- Art. 12: MTC and operators run awareness campaigns in the quarter after the adaptation period.
- Art. 15: public "Registro de Asignacion de la Serie 500" kept by DGPPC, published on the MTC digital site.
- Scope of "commercial": promoting or offering goods, services, brands, promotions or benefits, including calls by third parties on behalf of a company (Gestion 17 Sep 2026: https://gestion.pe/economia/llamadas-comerciales-en-peru-empezaran-con-500-carta-libre-para-que-empresas-te-contacten-noticia/).
- Calls AND SMS covered. WhatsApp, apps, robocalls/recorded calls: NOT mentioned in any source; EVV says other digital channels are outside (https://www.evv.pe/asignacion-de-numeracion-500-a-las-empresas-para-realizar-llamadas-con-fines-publicitarios/). Robocalls: UNVERIFIED (they are calls, so presumably covered if commercial).
- Informative calls (security alerts, appointment confirmations, delivery notices, outage info) may stay on normal numbers; if a promotion is added, they become commercial; promotions to existing customers are commercial (Gestion, experts EY Law and Olaechea). EVV flags service/loyalty/retention/post-sale calls for review.
- Responsibility for an infraction by a call center falls on the company (EVV; secondary).
- Who is formally bound: the DS is addressed to OPERATORS (telcos). Companies are bound in practice because operators can only give commercial numbering in the 500 series, and companies fall under Indecopi consumer rules. Wording of any direct company obligation: UNVERIFIED in the decree text; Gestion says the new infractions "mainly target telecom operators".

- 9 May 2025: Ley 32323 published. It gave the Executive 60 days to set special numbering (not met on time; Infobae notes it took over a year).
- 7 Feb 2026: DL 1723.

- Awareness campaign: quarter after adaptation (about Mar-Jun 2027).
- Within 18 months of entry into force (about Mar 2028): MTC evaluates the 500 series and may keep, change or add conditions (3rd final provision). MTC may add other series if 500 runs out.
- Companies do not have to switch immediately; the date is for operators (EVV). Whether companies calling from other numbers after 15 Mar 2027 are directly sanctioned: UNVERIFIED. One press item (APNoticias, quoting the vice-minister) says calls without the prefix would be prohibited and reportable: https://www.apnoticias.pe/adios-a-las-llamadas-spam-usuarios-podran-denunciar-publicidad-telefonica-que-no-tenga-el-prefijo-500-1516151

## 4. Related rules
- Art. 58.1(e) Codigo del Consumidor (Ley 32323, 9 May 2025): call centers, call systems, SMS/electronic messages and telemarketing to promote products/services prohibited unless the consumer gave consent "libre, previo, informado, expreso e inequivoco"; revocable at any time without cause; the "primer contacto" (an unsolicited first call to ask for consent) is no longer allowed. Source: El Peruano above; CMS https://cms.law/en/per/publication/promulgan-ley-contra-llamadas-y-mensajes-spam. Congreso note: https://comunicaciones.congreso.gob.pe/damos-cuenta/publican-ley-32323-que-pone-punto-final-a-las-llamadas-spam
- Conflict: the reglamento of the data law (DS 016-2024-JUS) admitted a promotional first contact; El Peruano says Ley 32323 prevails.
- Art. 58.3 (new): the State regulates messages/calls on telecom networks (basis for the 500 numbering).
- "Gracias, no insista" registry: was replaced by the prior-consent rule through DL 1390 (2018), per PUCP Themis paper https://revistas.pucp.edu.pe/index.php/themis/article/download/24885/24929?inline=1 (secondary). Some sites still describe it as active (modelo.pe), likely outdated. Current status: UNVERIFIED with official Indecopi source.
- Ley 29733 (data protection), authority ANPD. Misuse of personal data -> ANPD complaint (https://trendperu.com/donde-reportar-llamadas-spam-peru-osiptel-2026/).
- Allowed calling hours: NOT found in any source. Ley 32323 / art. 58 article says nothing on hours (El Peruano analysis). UNVERIFIED; do not state any hour range.

## 5. Sanctions and enforcement
- Calling without consent (art. 58.1(e)): "muy grave", up to 450 UIT (about S/ 2.4M); failure to cooperate with inspection up to 50 UIT. Source: Gestion 24 Sep 2026 https://gestion.pe/peru/llamadas-spam-en-peru-por-que-aun-persisten-y-que-medidas-hacen-falta-para-eliminarlas-noticia/ ; Indecopi presentation to Congress July 2025 (via search summary; the PDF URL returned 404).
- Telco obligations under DS 018 (DS art. 14 + new numeral 27 of art. 258 of DS 020-2007-MTC): very serious infractions for (a) using a new block without 70% usage of the previous one, (b) carrying commercial calls/SMS without identification/routing/traceability, (c) not showing the full 500 number. Enforced by MTC (DGFSC) under DS 028-2019-MTC. UIT amounts for these: not stated in sources, UNVERIFIED.
- Who enforces what: Indecopi = no consent (companies); MTC = numbering/operators; ANPD = personal data misuse; OSIPTEL = telecom service issues only, has no powers over company advertising calls (https://www.gob.pe/institucion/osiptel/noticias/1435217-recibes-llamadas-spam-osiptel-explica-donde-reportar-estos-casos).
- Fines (all Indecopi):
  - BBVA Peru: 279.50 UIT = S/ 1,537,250, first instance, Jan 2026, appealed (https://larepublica.pe/economia/2026/01/26/indecopi-sanciona-al-banco-bbva-con-15-millones-de-soles-por-realizar-llamadas-spam-no-autorizadas-evat-2274298 ; https://www.peru-retail.com/indecopi-multa-a-bbva-con-s-1-5-millones-por-llamadas-spam-bcp-e-interbank-en-la-mira/).
  - Interbank: 74.88 UIT = S/ 411,840, first instance, 18 Aug 2026 (https://www.gob.pe/institucion/indecopi/noticias/1431904-el-indecopi-dispone-sancionar-a-entidad-bancaria-con-mas-de-s-400-000-por-llamadas-spam).
  - Pacifico Seguros: S/ 118,195, confirmed on appeal, Aug 2026; over 11,000 audios analysed (https://www.infobae.com/peru/2026/08/05/aseguradora-debera-pagar-s-118195-por-llamadas-spam-se-analizaron-mas-de-11-mil-audios-para-determinar-la-multa/ ; https://www.gob.pe/institucion/indecopi/noticias/1426675-en-segunda-instancia-el-indecopi-confirma-sancion-contra-pacifico-seguros-por-llamadas-spam).
  - BBVA 11.6 UIT (S/ 63,800) for one advertising call, confirmed by Sala, June 2026 (search summary only, UNVERIFIED primary).
  - Earlier: BBVA and Rimac, more than 600,000 calls (Caretas, 2025: https://caretas.pe/?p=756938), not checked in detail. BCP under investigation. Integratel sanctioned for not delivering audio recordings (Gestion headline only).

## 8. Implications for MYPEs selling by phone (derived; the legal base is Ley 32323 + DS 018)
- The 500 prefix does not authorize calls and does not prove consent (trendperu, EVV, Gestion). The main rule remains prior consent that the consumer gave on their own initiative; cold calling and first contact to ask for consent are not allowed.
- Practical: outbound promotional calls and SMS will have to go out from a 500 number provided by an operator/provider after about March 2027. How a small business obtains one (through which operator, cost, availability): UNVERIFIED, none of the operators had published offers in the sources found.
- Expect lower pick-up rates: experts warn recognizable 500 calls will be rejected (Gestion, Mejia).
- Keep proof of consent (who, when, how), allow revocation, keep records of which number/agency called; review contracts with call centers (company bears responsibility).
- Alternatives (general, not from a norm): inbound leads from web forms with explicit consent checkbox; WhatsApp Business only with opt-in and template rules (the DS does not mention WhatsApp; Ley 32323 art. 58 covers "mensajes electronicos"); calling people who contacted you first; service/transactional calls with no promotion stay on normal numbers.
