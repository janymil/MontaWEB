# SUNAT and sales on Instagram/WhatsApp (DS 058-2026-EF): go/no-go research
Date: 2026-11-27 · Verdict: **no-go** as an article (decided: `docs/decisions/2026-11-27-sunat-ventas-redes-article.md`)

## The pitch
"SUNAT can now inspect sales on Instagram and WhatsApp (DS 058-2026-EF)." It would sell websites and online shops to people who sell through social media.

## Facts (sources checked 2026-11-27; the official El Peruano text could not be opened, so the summary relies on press and law firms)
| Claim | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|
| DS 058-2026-EF exists | VERIFIED. It is a MEF decree published 15-04-2026 and in force 16-04-2026: "Modifican Reglamento del fedatario fiscalizador para fiscalizar ventas por internet". It amends DS 086-2003-EF and is based on DL 1523 and Código Tributario art. 165. | Gestión 20-04-2026; IUS Latin 17-04-2026; El Peruano 294085 (22-04-2026) |
| What it allows | A SUNAT inspector (fedatario) can **pose as a buyer online**: make the purchase, pay by any means, and check whether a comprobante is issued (CT art. 174 num. 1–3). The inspector identifies themselves afterwards through the seller's buzón electrónico, and the electronic acta is valid proof. | IUS Latin; bybconsultores.pe; Infobae 17-04-2026 |
| SUNAT "inspects" or monitors Instagram/WhatsApp | FALSE as a description of the decree. It creates no monitoring of posts and no reporting duty for Meta, Yape, Plin or banks. It creates no new obligations for sellers either: the existing duty to issue a comprobante is applied to online sales. | Infobae; Jorge Picón in Gestión |
| Penalties | The fines already in the Código Tributario: 25%/50% of a UIT, 0.3% of income, or closure (UIT 2026 = S/ 5,500) | Gestión |
| RUC must appear in social-media ads | NOT in the decree. A separate earlier SUNAT rule (date unverified) | Canal N |
| Real enforcement cases (undercover purchases, fines, operations) | **NOT FOUND** by 27-11-2026. Gestión expects the effect on collection in 1–2 years. | — |
| Yape/Plin reporting | No 2026 threshold rule was found. SUNAT's proposal to count wallet receipts as boletas is "en evaluación" | Infobae 18-05-2026 |

## Judgment
1. **Legal text:** real, but much narrower than the pitch. It allows undercover purchases, nothing more. A headline saying "SUNAT revisa tus ventas en Instagram" would overstate the law, the same mistake as precio-por-interno.
2. **Case:** no enforcement case 7 months after the decree took effect.
3. **Demand:** the news peak has passed, and searches for the problem are about zero. The large evergreen queries (boleta electrónica, RUS) are SUNAT tutorials that don't lead to buying a website.
4. **Fit:** weak. Whether a seller gets fined depends on issuing a comprobante, not on having a website. The honest message would be "emite boleta". MontaWeb doesn't sell that.

## What can be reused (no new article)
- One FAQ line on /tiendas-virtuales, worded accurately: since April 2026 SUNAT can check online sales by buying as a customer, so issue a comprobante for every sale. Before using it, read the official text of the decree.
- Reconsider if SUNAT publishes the first results of these undercover purchases (counts, fines), or if the Yape/Plin-as-boleta proposal becomes a norm.
