## Marketplace verdict: do not list

Meta's Commerce Policies (facebook.com/policies_center/commerce, read 2026-11-25), section "Services":
"Commerce content may not promote the buying, selling or trading of services, except on the WhatsApp Business app or through Appointments in Facebook or Instagram."
Web design is a service, so a Marketplace listing breaks the policy and risks losing commerce access for the account. **Nothing listed.** Use instead: the Page's Services tab (each plan with its price), the WhatsApp Business catalogue (allowed for services), and Appointments on the Page if Jan wants bookings.
