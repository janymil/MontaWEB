# YouTube RPM / CPM by niche and language (research, 2026-11-25)

Key finding: I found almost NO first-party creator data (Studio screenshots, Reddit threads) for these niches. Searches did not surface any r/PartneredYoutube threads, any AI-microdrama channel disclosure, or any Spanish-language creator stating their own RPM. Everything per-niche below is vendor estimate or second-hand unless marked. Nothing was invented; gaps are marked UNVERIFIED.

RPM = creator revenue per 1,000 total views after YouTube's cut. CPM = advertiser price per 1,000 ad impressions, before the cut (CPM overstates RPM).

## A. Datasets with methodology (strongest)
1. AIR Media-Tech, long-form RPM, 300 monetized channels, 3,595 channel-months, May 2025-May 2026, YouTube Analytics data; page updated 2026-09-24 / 2026-10-01. Median all niches $2.30. Education & Science median $10.22 (bottom quartile $2.31, top quartile $19.50); Entertainment $2.43. Geography not split. Type: dataset. https://air.io/en/monetization/creator-earnings-compared-youtube-vs-tiktok-vs-instagram-in-2026 and https://air.io/en/air-data-findings/youtube-monetization-statistics-2026
2. AIR Media-Tech, Shorts RPM, 274 channels, 3,044 channel-months, 12 months: $0.02-$1.48 per 1,000 views by niche; most niches $0.02-$0.20; US audiences about $0.33. Shorts = 3-14% of the same channel's long-form RPM (music 41-60%). So 1M Shorts views is roughly $20-$200 (derived by the page; US about $330). Same URLs as above. Type: dataset.
3. AIR Media-Tech, country CPM (not RPM), 3,000+ channels, last updated 2025-07-28: US $14.67 (June 2025 study), Chile $1.01, Mexico $0.91, Brazil $0.82, Uruguay $0.76, Colombia $0.62, Peru $0.61, Argentina $0.55. Spain not given. https://air.io/en/audience-growth/growing-your-youtube-audience-in-latam-mena-sea and the statistics page above. Type: dataset (CPM; RPM would be lower).
4. Dynamoi, Creator AdSense RPM, channels connected to Dynamoi (skews music/artist channels, not general creators), 2025-11-23 to 2026-11-22: Mexico $0.24 (rank 61 of 85), Peru $0.24, Colombia $0.23; all-market average $1.06, median $0.71, top market Australia $6.49. US and Spain not on the page. Mexico playback CPM $1.91. Another Dynamoi snapshot elsewhere showed Mexico $0.26 and $0.44, so unstable. https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/mx . Type: dataset, biased sample.

## C. Language / geography
- Spanish: no first-party creator RPM found. Anchors: AIR country CPM Peru $0.61, Mexico $0.91 vs US $14.67 (about 15-24x gap in CPM). Dynamoi (biased sample) Mexico/Peru RPM about $0.24 vs all-market avg $1.06.
- FluxNote (vendor, no methodology, 2026-06-26, https://fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-cpm-spain-2026): CPM Spain EUR 6-18 (finance 10-18, gaming 2-6), Mexico $2-8, US Hispanic $8-25, LatAm combined $2-8. These conflict with AIR's measured Mexico CPM $0.91 and are probably too high; use only for relative ordering (US Hispanic > Spain > Mexico/LatAm). UNVERIFIED.
- Implication: a Spanish channel whose audience is mostly Peru/Mexico/LatAm should expect RPM roughly 5-15% of the same content in US English; a US-Hispanic/Spain-weighted audience is much better but its size and RPM were not verified.
- Other: "Cuentos Facinantes" (US Spanish AI fairy tales, 9.73M subs) is cited, no RPM (HackerNoon link).

## D. Reliability warnings
- Most niche pages (FluxNote, Kineclip, Saturaai, etc.) are sellers of AI-channel tools with unsourced ranges; they conflict (history $4-15).
- Case studies like "$39,500/month AI channel" or "$75k" come from tool sellers; screenshots unverified.
- YouTube does not publish RPM by niche or country.
- YouTube announced higher monetization thresholds for February 2027 (reported by HackerNoon, 2026-10-02; not independently checked).
