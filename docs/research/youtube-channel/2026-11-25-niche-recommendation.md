## Short report for Jan
**Recommendation:** an English long-form documentary channel on ancient and pre-Columbian civilizations (Olmec, Maya, Aztec, Inca, Nazca, Chavín, Caral, Tiwanaku). Visuals are AI-generated, plus real photos and clips of the sites from stock and open-licence sources. One 25–35 min video a week plus 2 Shorts cut from it.

**Why:** among all the niches, this is the only one that meets all four conditions:
- New faceless channels break out in it this month. Ethos started 07.10.2026 and has 26.6k subs with a median of ~13k views per upload. EL ORIGEN, the same topic in Spanish, started 30.10.2026 and has 831k, 437k and 263k views. Both look AI-made, so a pure-AI channel can grow here.
- It sits in the highest-paying RPM category (education, AIR median $10.22, history estimates $4–10).
- Each episode is about a real place and real objects, so real photos of them are easy to get from stock and museum archives. That makes each video specific, not generic AI imagery.
- It fits one video a week.

**Biggest risk, in proportion:** YouTube does not ban AI content. Its "inauthentic content" rule targets channels that look mass-produced: the same template every time, high volume, little original work. AI voice and AI images are allowed; realistic AI scenes need the "altered or synthetic" disclosure, which YouTube says does not limit monetization. The risk is real in this niche because many channels upload near-daily with one title template, and review is at channel level. The plan avoids that pattern: one original script a week with its own structure, cited sources, real photos of the actual sites and artifacts mixed with the AI visuals, varied titles. The second risk is plain math: most videos of a new channel get 500–5,000 views, and the channel depends on one or two hits.

**Runner-up:** the same topic in Spanish. The view data is even stronger, but RPM is about 5–15x lower (Peru CPM $0.61 and Mexico $0.91 vs US $14.67, AIR), and every weekly script would need the full non-English writing process and a review by Jan.

## The recommended channel

**Working positioning:** "Lost civilizations of the Americas, explained with the evidence." English, with documentary narration. Each episode answers one question about one site or culture, uses current archaeology with cited sources, and shows real photos of the site and its artifacts next to the AI reconstructions.

- **Language:** English first, for RPM. Spanish later only as a separate decision. A dubbed track made from the English script would break the rule that English is never the template; YouTube's own auto-dubbing would need Jan's yes.
- **Scope:** all of the Americas, not only the Andes. The data shows Mesoamerica pulls more (in Spanish: Olmecs 831k, Teotihuacán 263k, Aztecs 236k vs Incas 27.5k).
- **Format:** 25–35 min, one topic, a clear question in the title, chapters, sources in the description, AI-content disclosure where realistic scenes are generated.
- **Cadence:** 1 long video a week + 2 Shorts (45–60 s, the best scene of the week) to the channel and to TikTok as traffic. No daily uploads, no compilations.
- **Visual rule:** AI reconstructions (stills with motion, a few Veo clips) for scenes nobody can film, and real photos/clips of the actual ruins, objects and maps for the evidence. Aim for roughly a quarter of screen time real. Never present an AI image as a real photo or a real "discovery".

## 90-day test with a stop rule

Start: the week Jan publishes video 1. Count days from that upload.

| Checkpoint | Measure (YouTube Studio) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 (4 videos) | CTR, average view duration (AVD) | If AVD < 25% on all 4: change script structure (shorter intro, question up front) before video 5. No stop yet. |
| Day 60 (8 videos) | best video's views, AVD | If no video > 2,000 views: switch the next 4 topics to Mesoamerica/"mystery" angles, the scan's strongest topics. |
| **Day 90 (12 videos)** | channel totals | **Continue** if at least one video ≥ 25,000 views **or** channel total ≥ 75,000 views, **and** AVD ≥ 30% on long videos. **Stop** if the median of videos 7–12 is < 1,000 views and no video passed 10,000. Anything in between: one more 45-day block, all on the topic family of the best video, then decide again on the same numbers. |

For scale: YPP needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours. At 30 min and 30% AVD (9 min per view), 4,000 hours ≈ 27,000 views, so the "continue" bar is roughly the YPP bar.

Money once monetized, at $4–10 RPM, with US share and 30% withholding on US earnings both affecting it: 100k views/month ≈ $300–800 after withholding (rough). The scan's typical new channel would be below that in its first months.
