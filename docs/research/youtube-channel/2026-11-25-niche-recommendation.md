# YouTube channel niche: ranked comparison and recommendation (2026-11-25)

Sources: channel data from a real-browser scan ([2026-11-25-channel-data.md](2026-11-25-channel-data.md)), RPM evidence ([2026-11-25-rpm-by-niche.md](2026-11-25-rpm-by-niche.md)), policy and tool terms ([2026-11-25-policy-and-tools.md](2026-11-25-policy-and-tools.md)), and the earlier work in `docs/research/ai-media-income/`.

## Short report for Jan
**Recommendation:** an English long-form documentary channel on ancient and pre-Columbian civilizations (Olmec, Maya, Aztec, Inca, Nazca, Chavín, Caral, Tiwanaku), with real footage Jan films at Peruvian sites. One 25–35 min video a week plus 2 Shorts cut from it.

**Why:** among all the niches, this is the only one that meets all four conditions:
- New faceless channels break out in it this month. Ethos started 07.10.2026 and has 26.6k subs with a median of ~13k views per upload. EL ORIGEN, the same topic in Spanish, started 30.10.2026 and has 831k, 437k and 263k views.
- It sits in the highest-paying RPM category (education, AIR median $10.22, history estimates $4–10).
- A Peru-based creator has something the AI-made competitors don't: his own footage of the places.
- It fits one video a week.

**Biggest risk:** the niche is filling up with mass-produced AI history channels. YouTube demonetizes whole channels for "inauthentic content", judged on the last ~30 uploads. A channel that looks like them (template titles, AI stills only, daily uploads) can lose monetization even after it grows. The countermeasures are part of the plan: own footage, original structure for each episode, cited sources, one upload a week. The second risk is plain math: most videos of a new channel get 500–5,000 views, and the channel depends on one or two hits.

**Runner-up:** the same topic in Spanish. The view data is even stronger, but RPM is about 5–15x lower (Peru CPM $0.61 and Mexico $0.91 vs US $14.67, AIR), and every weekly script would need the full non-English writing process and a review by Jan.

## The recommended channel

**Working positioning:** "Lost civilizations of the Americas, told from where they stood." English, with documentary narration. Each episode answers one question about one site or culture, uses current archaeology with cited sources, and includes Jan's own footage when the site is reachable from Lima (Caral, Pachacamac, Lima's huacas; later Chan Chan, Cusco, Nazca).

- **Language:** English first, for RPM. Spanish later only as a separate decision. A dubbed track made from the English script would break the rule that English is never the template; YouTube's own auto-dubbing would need Jan's yes.
- **Scope:** all of the Americas, not only the Andes. The data shows Mesoamerica pulls more (in Spanish: Olmecs 831k, Teotihuacán 263k, Aztecs 236k vs Incas 27.5k). Andean episodes bring the footage advantage.
- **Format:** 25–35 min, one topic, a clear question in the title, chapters, sources in the description, AI-content disclosure where realistic scenes are generated.
- **Cadence:** 1 long video a week + 2 Shorts (45–60 s, the best scene of the week) to the channel and to TikTok as traffic. No daily uploads, no compilations.
- **Visual rule:** at least 20% of screen time is real footage (Jan's own, or public-domain/CC archive), the rest AI stills with motion, maps and a few Veo clips. Never show a fake "discovery" as real.

### Time per video (target ≤ 9 h)
Research + outline with Claude 2 h · script edit 1.5 h · voice 0.5 h · visuals 2.5 h · edit 2 h · thumbnail + upload 0.5 h. Weeks with a site visit add filming time, but that footage covers several episodes.

## 90-day test with a stop rule

Start: the week Jan publishes video 1. Count days from that upload.

| Checkpoint | Measure (YouTube Studio) | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 (4 videos) | CTR, average view duration (AVD) | If AVD < 25% on all 4: change script structure (shorter intro, question up front) before video 5. No stop yet. |
| Day 60 (8 videos) | best video's views, AVD | If no video > 2,000 views: switch the next 4 topics to Mesoamerica/"mystery" angles, the scan's strongest topics. |
| **Day 90 (12 videos)** | channel totals | **Continue** if at least one video ≥ 25,000 views **or** channel total ≥ 75,000 views, **and** AVD ≥ 30% on long videos. **Stop** if the median of videos 7–12 is < 1,000 views and no video passed 10,000. Anything in between: one more 45-day block, all on the topic family of the best video, then decide again on the same numbers. |

For scale: YPP needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours. At 30 min and 30% AVD (9 min per view), 4,000 hours ≈ 27,000 views, so the "continue" bar is roughly the YPP bar.

Money once monetized, at $4–10 RPM, with US share and 30% withholding on US earnings both affecting it: 100k views/month ≈ $300–800 after withholding (rough). The scan's typical new channel would be below that in its first months.
