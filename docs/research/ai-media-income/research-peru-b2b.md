## 4. WhatsApp and ads
- Template header types (Meta docs, fetched today, https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/components): TEXT; MEDIA = IMAGE, VIDEO, GIF (Marketing Messages API only), DOCUMENT; LOCATION. AUDIO IS NOT LISTED. So an audio header in a marketing template is not supported per docs. Audio/voice messages are a separate message type (https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/audio-messages: voice = .ogg OPUS, mono, <=16 MB, `voice:true`). Non-template messages only while the 24-hour customer-service window is open (https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing/). Implication: proactive outbound voice notes via API cannot be a marketing template; only inside a 24h window (after customer wrote) or via free-entry point (CTWA ads; pricing page says FEP window up to 7 days, not 72 h as one blog claimed).
- Business app broadcast lists: help page (via search snippet, official faq page URLs returned 404 for me) says up to 256 contacts per list and recipients must have saved your number; messages to others silently fail. Voice notes in broadcast: no source confirms or denies. UNVERIFIED; test it. Practical limit: only existing contacts who saved the number.
- Marketing template per-message price for Peru: exists in Meta rate card (CSV/PDF), figure not read. Unverified.
- Click-to-WhatsApp ads in Peru: no Peru adoption statistic found (only agency blogs, 77% of consumers use WhatsApp for shopping per an Accenture/Facebook study cited by Andina, https://andina.pe/agencia/noticia-whatsapp-y-gobierno-del-peru-se-unen-para-apoyar-recuperacion-pymes-866052.aspx). Adoption % unverified.

## Assessment
- Market range observed for AI video in Lima: S/49-400 for single clips/small packs; S/790-1,790/mo for Kozma 360 retainers (4-12 videos, IGV included). Human community management for micro/small: S/500-1,200/mo; agencies S/2,000+. S/1,490+IGV (S/1,758) for 8 videos + 4 audios sits above Kozma's 8-video plan (S/1,290 incl. IGV, i.e., ~S/1,093 net) and ~2-3x the typical micro-business CM budget. Micro businesses paying S/50-99 for a website are unlikely buyers at that level. Lower/unproven ground: audio retainer pricing (no market data).
