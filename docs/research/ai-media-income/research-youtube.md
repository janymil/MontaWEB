## 3. Enforcement

- 2025-07-15: YouTube renamed "repetitious content" to "inauthentic content" in YPP policy (mass-produced / repetitive). YouTube said it was a clarification, not a ban on AI or faceless channels (PlagiarismToday 2025-07-08; Windows Central).
- 2025-12-27: Screen Culture and KH Studio (AI fake movie trailers, ~1B views combined) terminated after earlier monetization restrictions (Deadline via https://loudest.in/news/youtube-removes-fake-ai-trailers-prompting-fears-of-ai-music-takedowns-18898.html).
- Early Jan 2026: Neal Mohan letter lists reducing "low quality AI content" as 2026 priority (Tubefilter 2026-01-29).
- 2026-01-12: creators report daily "inauthentic content" demonetization notices; one claims ~$40k December earnings at risk; networks of 10+ automated channels most hit; TeamYouTube defined it as mass-produced / template-based; appeals suggested; some reinstated after human review. No official sweep announcement. https://piunikaweb.com/2026/01/12/youtube-inauthentic-content-policy-enforcement-reports/ Also late 2025 AI moderation errors on Minecraft/Roblox channels; YouTube admitted (Nov 10 2025) using AI in appeals.
- 2026-01 (Kapwing/Tubefilter): 16 of the top 100 slop channels (35M subs, 4.7B views, ~$9.8-10M/yr est.) deleted/wiped, at least 18 per Tubefilter, incl. Cuentos Facinantes and Imperio de Jesus. https://tubefilter.com/2026/01/29/youtube-ai-slop-channel-crackdown-bans/
- 2026-06-15 (TNW): enforcement is channel-level (last ~30 uploads can demonetize whole channel); faceless voiceover explainers and ambient videos reportedly collateral; creator Doctor NOS (1.7M) says most faceless imitators are demonetized (unverified claim); YouTube auto-labels AI content (C2PA, SynthID); AI-labelled content not penalized per se. https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-faceless-creators-collateral-damage
- 2026-07-13: YouTube clarified three buckets (Tubefilter): (1) generic/repetitive "made with a template" (slideshows, minimal commentary, templated storylines); (2) unsatisfying/off-putting (emotionally manipulative formulas, AI clips with no logical arc, fake disaster/celebrity-death visuals); (3) AI personas on sensitive topics (AI "doctor/lawyer/financial host") not allowed to monetize. "Not a new policy." https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/
- 2026-08-05 (Engadget): Matt Halprin (VP Trust & Safety) on Creator Insider: YouTube "agnostic" about tools; effort and creativity required; applies only to YPP monetization. https://engadget.com/2226445/youtube-explains-ai-slop-policy-why-some-creators-wont-get-paid/
- Reapply after 90 days after rejection (metricool/secondary).
- Hackernoon (Octo Browser employee, promotional): YPP tiers 500 subs/3 videos/3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views; "YouTube plans to tighten thresholds Feb 2027" (unverified).
- Counts of demonetized channels in 2025: no verified figure found. Formats clearly hit: AI kids/fairy-tale slop, religious AI slop, fake trailers, brainrot Shorts, multi-channel automation farms. True-crime-with-AI-persona falls under bucket 3 only if presented as expert advice; no documented AI true-crime wave found.

## 4. AI music / sleep music

- No official YouTube statement specifically on AI music or sleep music monetization found. Halprin/Tubefilter wording covers "static images + generic content" generally.
- Creator-education vendors (JackRighteous, OutlierKit; commercial, weak evidence) report YPP rejections for AI tracks over static image/slideshow with no editing; they say lofi/sleep/meditation is where repetitiveness issues concentrate; other pages argue curated lofi live streams are not flagged. No named channel rejection case with a link found. https://jackrighteous.com/blogs/ai-music-distribution-guide/can-you-monetize-ai-music-on-youtube-in-2026

- Suno/ElevenLabs Music licensing for commercial YouTube use not checked here; verify plan terms.

## Conclusions
- Verified traction for NEW Spanish AI channels in 2025-26: not established. The only large Spanish AI channels with data were slop channels, mostly removed Jan 2026.
- Higher-RPM niches (education/science/history, true crime) show better pay but must add real narrative/script, not templates. Kids and music lowest.
- Biggest risk: channel-level inauthentic-content demonetization, hitting multi-channel automation and template formats; a single well-edited channel with original scripts is the stated safe path (YouTube statements above).
