# AI audio / music income research (as of 2026-11-25)
Method: web searches + page fetches. Vendor blogs are marked as such. "Unverified" = could not confirm on a primary source.

## 1. ElevenLabs Voice Library payouts
- Total paid to voice creators: >$22M cumulative, 10,400+ creators (was $11M in Nov 2025). ElevenLabs blog, published 2026-05-22, updated 2026-11-03: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/22-million-earned-by-voice-creators-on-elevenlabs . Company-reported. Derived average = ~$2,100 per creator cumulative (my arithmetic; distribution surely very skewed).
- Rates: default ~1.1 cents per 1,000 characters, up to 3 cents per 1k chars; creators set price tier + notice period (immediate to 2 years); cash or character credits. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/blog/how-much-do-voice-actors-make (pub 2024-03-27, updated 2026-09-22). Docs say default rate depends on notice period; no per-1k figure on docs page: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/payouts
- Derived math: $1,000/month at 1.1-3 cents/1k chars needs ~33-90M paid characters/month used by OTHERS (my arithmetic).
- Top earner: ~$4k/month (March 2024, ElevenLabs blog above). A secondary site claims some >$10k/month (https://novyny.live/en/tehnologii/some-ai-voice-donors-receive-up-to-usd-10000-per-month-257281.html) - unverified. Named examples (Simon Patrick full-time; no figures) in the $22M post. No individual payout screenshots/Reddit data found.
- Earnings only from usage by PAID users, not free users. Payout every 6-8 days via Stripe Connect, min threshold usually $10.
- Requirements: Professional Voice Clone (PVC) -> needs Creator plan or higher to create; paid sub NOT required to receive payouts; ~2 h clean audio recommended (official marketing says 30 min minimum); on-camera/voice-captcha verification; team review before listing; Stripe Connect account; W-8/W-9 tax form (US withholding may apply).
- PERU IS on the Stripe Connect supported list (also Slovakia, Czech Republic, Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Chile, Argentina). Docs: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-creative/voices/payouts
- Slovak/Czech supply and demand: NOT FOUND. Voice Library has 32 languages (per $22M post); India/Hindi named as fastest growing; no per-language demand data published. ElevenLabs language pages feature a few Czech voices (Petr Sovadina, Iveta, Marketa, Zoe) and one Slovak (Peter): https://elevenlabs.io/languages/czech , https://elevenlabs.io/languages/slovak . Supply looks thin by those pages, but thin supply != demand; demand is unverified. A founder cannot assume paid use. Check Voice Library filtered by language manually.
- Also: ElevenLabs "licensing deals" with guaranteed up-front fees exist for professional voice actors (same blog) - details unverified.

## 2. AI music distribution and earnings
Spotify (primary: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-09-25/spotify-strengthens-ai-protections/ , 2025-09-25): 75M+ spam tracks removed in 12 months; impersonation policy (vocal clone only with artist authorization); music spam filter (mass uploads, duplicates, SEO hacks, very short tracks -> tagged and not recommended); DDEX AI credits standard (vocals/instrumentation/post-production), disclosed tracks not penalized; partners incl. DistroKid, CD Baby, Believe, FUGA. Later update: AI credits beta from April 16 (year not stated, likely 2026), tens of thousands of credits daily; disclosure voluntary. Fully-AI tracks are NOT banned by Spotify.
- Spotify pay: ~$0.003-0.005 per stream (TuneCore guide https://tunecore.com/guides/how-much-does-spotify-pay ; ~$4.43/1000 US streams Jan 2026 from a tracker, secondary); 1,000-stream minimum per track per year (since April 2024) plus unique-listener minimum.
Apple Music: "Transparency Tags" metadata launched March 2026 (optional): https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/05/apple-music-ai-transparency-tags/ ; Aug 20, 2026 email says tags REQUIRED when AI used for a material portion, "Made With AI" labels later in 2026 (https://macrumors.com/2026/08/20/apple-music-to-label-ai-generated-songs ). Exact effective date unclear.
Deezer: AI = 44% of daily uploads (Apr 2026), >50% / >90k tracks/day (June 2026, TechCrunch 2026-07-21 https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/21/music-streamer-deezer-says-more-than-50-of-daily-uploads-are-ai-generated/ ), but only ~1-3% of streams; ~85% of those streams detected fraudulent and demonetized; removes AI tracks tied to fraud and tracks unplayed for 6 months (per search summary). Key signal: oversupply, near-zero organic demand.
Distributors (mostly secondary sources, check each help centre):
- DistroKid: accepts AI music if you hold commercial rights, disclose AI use at upload (vocals/lyrics/melody/instrumentation), no impersonation/sound-alike covers. Help article reportedly updated 2026-08-19 (secondary: https://dynamoi.com/learn/ai-music-distribution/does-distrokid-accept-ai-music , https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/05/14/distrokid-ai-credit-disclosure-what-it-looks-like/ ). Retroactive removal claim: unverified.
- TuneCore: rejects fully AI-generated; AI-assisted with significant human input judged case by case (secondary: https://undetectr.com/blog/distrokid-tunecore-cdbaby-ai-policies-compared ).
- CD Baby: strictest, removes fully AI tracks, possible termination (same source; Hypebot https://www.hypebot.com/are-tunecore-cd-baby-distrokid-symphonic-blocking-ai-music.md ). Note: CD Baby is also a Spotify DDEX partner. Conflicting secondary info; verify.
Suno (primary help page https://help.suno.com/en/articles/13614785 ; blog https://suno.com/blog/suno-updates-tos ; ToS rev. Aug 10 2026, effective Sep 3 2026 per search summary, https://suno.com/terms-september-2026 not read):
- Download caps from 2026-09-03: Free = 7 lifetime trial downloads, NON-commercial; Pro = 20/month; Premier = 60/month (extra purchasable); Premier on Studio unlimited downloads. Paid-plan downloads carry commercial rights; no retroactive license for free songs. Ownership wording softened after Warner deal (subscribers "generally not considered owner", Music in Africa https://www.musicinafrica.net/magazine/suno-adjusts-ai-music-ownership-terms-after-warner-music-partnership , Nov 2025). Suno-Warner settlement 2025-11-25 (Rolling Stone, Billboard). New licensed models planned for 2026 (launch not confirmed by me; a secondary source claims v6 announced 2026-09-09 - unverified). Implication: ~20-60 distributable tracks/month; copyright ownership of AI output is legally weak/unregistered in many jurisdictions (general note, no source).
Udio: UMG settlement 2025-10-29, Warner 2025-11; downloads disabled immediately (48-hour reprieve), walled garden; new licensed platform (report: "Starstruck", MBW May 2026) not confirmed launched; no commercial-use terms found. Treat Udio as DEAD END for distribution. https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/universal-music-settles-udio-lawsuit-strikes-deal-for-licensed-ai-music-platform
ElevenLabs Eleven Music (terms updated 2026-10-09, https://elevenlabs.io/eleven-music-model-specific-terms ): streaming distribution PERMITTED on Creator plan and above (Free/Starter prohibited); media rights broad except film/TV/radio/studio games on non-enterprise plans; Creator 250 min/mo downloads; no music libraries/model resale; you retain rights in output; no artist-name prompts; follow each platform's AI disclosure. Content ID not addressed. Licensed with Merlin and Kobalt (opt-in). This is the cleanest commercial license of the three.
Real earnings data: Velvet Sundown (AI band, 1M+ monthly listeners, July 2025) estimated "up to ~$2,000/month" by press, no royalty statement (https://www.joe.co.uk/news/seemingly-ai-band-racks-up-400000-monthly-listeners-less-than-month-spotify-493956 , https://dataconomy.com/2025/07/17/ai-bands-are-now-topping-the-charts-and-earning-real-money ) - unverified and an outlier built on virality. ## 3. YouTube Content ID and AI music
- No primary YouTube statement found barring AI tracks from Content ID; Content ID access normally via distributors/aggregators for original works, and false-claim abuse is a documented problem (dtptips article https://dtptips.com/ai-music-copyright-nightmare-how-ai-tracks-are-abusing-content-id-and-threatening-creators ; vendor blogs, low reliability). Risks: tool provider/other uploaders registering the same or similar output, false positives, claims on your own tracks by lookalike AI tracks, termination from distributors, pending training-data litigation. Eleven Music terms silent on Content ID. Suno blog claims AI tracks reduce claim risk (vendor claim). Conclusion: registering AI tracks is possible only via a distributor that offers Content ID and accepts AI (verify per distributor); high dispute risk; no verified income data.
