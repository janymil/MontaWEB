## (a) Ley 31814 + Reglamento: AI labelling

**Conclusion: the current copy is inaccurate.** Neither the reglamento nor the law requires a small agency to label AI promo videos.
- **Norm:** D.S. 115-2025-PCM (Reglamento de la Ley 31814), signed 08.09.2025 and published in El Peruano 09.09.2025. It enters into force 90 días hábiles after publication (Disposición Complementaria Final Única), which law firms report as **22.01.2026** (⚠️ that date is from a law firm, not the decree). No later amendment found.
- **Art. 25.1 (verified):** the duty applies only to *"El desarrollador o implementador de un sistema basado en IA de riesgo alto"*. That person must set up algorithmic-transparency mechanisms.
- **Art. 25.2 (verified):** labelling is optional: *"Entre los mecanismos se puede incluir … el etiquetado visible"*. A label, when used, should say that a product, service or content *"se opera con base en IA"*.
- **High risk (arts. 22.1.b, 24.1):** high-risk uses are those that threaten life, dignity or liberty, or that involve critical services, credit scoring, hiring, etc. **AI-made promo reels are not high risk.** MontaWeb is at most an "implementador" (art. 6.d), and AI content is not an art. 25 duty.
- **Timetable (1st Disp. Compl. Final, verified):** art. 25 applies to the private sector gradually, starting from 10.09.2025. Microempresas (≤150 UIT) get **3 years**, pequeñas empresas (150–1,700 UIT) get 2 years, and "other uses" get 4 years. So even a high-risk system run by a micro would not be bound before ~09.2028.
- **Ley 31814 itself:** sets principles (transparency among them, art. 7.i of the reglamento: systems should be "claros, explicables y accesibles"). It sets no labelling rule and no fines. The reglamento has no sanctions article: SGTD-PCM supervises (art. 8.g) and refers cases to Indecopi / ANPD (arts. 34.2, 36).
- **Platforms (⚠️ secondary or own-policy sources, not law):** TikTok asks creators to label AI content with realistic images, audio or video. Meta requires its "AI info" disclosure for photorealistic video or realistic audio in organic posts and may apply penalties.
- **What really makes the label useful in Peru:** D.Leg. 1044 art. 8 (actos de engaño, in KB) and CPDC arts. 2–3: an ad must not mislead about the product. The label plus "real prices / no invented testimonials" is good practice against engaño. **«Video generado con IA» is enough** as a label: it is visible, early and plain.
- **Demo label** «Ejemplo con un negocio ficticio · Hecho con IA»: keep it. It avoids any implied link with a real business (D.Leg. 1044 art. 10, riesgo de asociación). Check that the fictitious names don't match real Lima businesses.

**Replacement es-PE text** (scanned with humanize-scan, 0 tells after removing «Además»; Jan should read it before publishing):

## (b) Prices: total with IGV

**Conclusion: yes. Every price shown on the site must be the final price with IGV, including prices aimed at businesses.**
- **CPDC (Ley 29571) art. 4.1 (verified, original text 02.09.2010):** when a supplier shows prices "en … listas de precios, rótulos, letreros…", it *"debe indicar en forma destacada el precio total … el cual debe incluir los tributos, comisiones y cargos aplicables"*. Art. 4.2: no extra charges unless they are for separate services, are disclosed beforehand and are expressly accepted. A web page is a price list (KB: art. 58.2 extends this to distance selling ⚠️ secondary). Art. 3 bans information that misleads about price.
- **Who counts as a consumer (art. IV.1.2, in KB):** microempresarios count as consumers when there is information asymmetry about products outside their own line of business. A bodega or a restaurant buying a promo video or a website fits that.
- **Even for pure B2B buyers:** D.Leg. 1044 art. 17.3.b (verified, local copy) treats it as a breach of the principle of legality in advertising to *"omitir la presentación del precio total … sin incluir los tributos aplicables"* whenever a price is advertised. That applies to every audience. Indecopi applies legality literally (Res. 0161-2019/SDC, USD-only price sanctioned). Fines can reach 700 UIT (art. 52), and a first case is usually leve.
- **No norm requires** the words "IGV incluido". What is required is that the number shown is the total. Saying so explicitly removes doubt for B2B buyers, who often assume "+ IGV".
- **/servicios today:** the cards say «pago único» and give no hint about IGV. If the amounts already include IGV, the cards are legal but ambiguous. If they don't, they breach CPDC 4.1 and D.Leg. 1044 17.3.b, and Jan must raise them by 18%.

**Recommended wording:** one line under each price block heading (web plans, extras, packs, monthly plans, videos), not on every card: «Precios finales en soles, con IGV incluido.» Keep «pago único» on the cards. For monthly items use «S/ 39 al mes». On the video page: «S/ 149 · precio final con IGV». Use the same line in the JSON-LD/FAQ text where prices appear.

## (c) Tax regime: can SEMANTIKA TECH E.I.R.L. invoice video/advertising work?

**Conclusion: yes under RMT or the Régimen General. It cannot be in Nuevo RUS, and RER is closed to this business anyway.**
- **RUC:** 20616806981 (as published on montaweb.pe; matches `Legal/_kb/procedimientos/tributario-sunat.md`). The KB file calls the company "S.A.C.", but the website and contracts say **E.I.R.L.** Fix the KB. The regime and the registered CIIU activities **could not be confirmed**: SUNAT's Consulta RUC is behind a captcha and no public mirror returned data.
- **Nuevo RUS:** only for personas naturales and sucesiones indivisas, so an E.I.R.L. is out. It also excludes *"Agencias de viaje, propaganda y/o publicidad"* and cannot issue facturas (SUNAT guide, verified).
- **RER (LIR art. 118.b, verified on sunat.gob.pe):** (vi) excludes *"titulares de agencias de viaje, propaganda y/o publicidad"*. (x.6) also excludes *"Programación informática, consultoría de informática y actividades conexas"*, which is the web agency's core activity. So the company should not be in RER at all.
- **RMT (≤1,700 UIT) or Régimen General:** no exclusion for advertising or audiovisual production. The company issues facturas and boletas with IGV 18% (16% IGV + 2% IPM), so «IGV incluido» is correct: S/ 149 = S/ 126.27 + S/ 22.73 IGV.
- **⚠️ Not verified:** whether the RER and RMT texts changed in 2026 (a fusion of regimes is reported as only a project), and the RUC rule on how fast new activities must be registered.
