## 2. Clutch, GoodFirms and others: what the forms ask

- **Clutch**: free, about 10 minutes. Fields: company name, tagline, employees, minimum project size, hourly rate, website, location, contact, overview, service focus (percentages), industries, client focus, portfolio. No character limits published (unverified). Leaving hourly rate or minimum project blank drops the profile from filtered lists. Reviews are not needed to publish; clients can leave one through a free online form (phone interviews are a paid tier). "Verified" is paid (price conflicting, ~US$ 499/yr; unverified): skip.
- **GoodFirms**: free "$0 forever" listing; the research team calls 2–3 client references before ranking (reported ~23 % acceptance, 5–10 business days; unverified). Required: name, logo, address, website. PRO tier paid (conflicting prices): skip.
- **Sortlist**: Peruvian agencies have claimable pages; fees not public (unverified).
- **Portfolio**: we have no client work to show publicly yet, so the portfolio uses the four demo sites, each labelled "diseño de muestra, no es la web de un cliente". Never mark them as client projects: it would be false, and both sites check portfolio claims against references.

## 3. Google Business Profile

**State on 25 Nov 2026: no profile.** Google Maps "MontaWeb Lima" and Google "MontaWeb San Isidro" show no MontaWeb in Peru, and the repo links no profile (no maps.app, g.page or cid link). The only "MONTAWEB" on Maps is a Spanish agency in Sabadell/Barcelona (4,4 estrellas, 9 reviews) plus a second, empty "Montaweb" in Barcelona. "Semantika Tech" was not searched separately (unverified).

Fixes:
1. Create the profile as **MontaWeb**, primary category "Diseñador de sitios web", website `https://montaweb.pe` (no www, matching the canonical-host decision).
2. Decide storefront vs service area: if clients don't visit San Isidro, hide the address and set Lima Metropolitana + Callao as the service area. A visible address nobody can visit gets suspended.
3. Verify (usually by video: workspace, RUC document, site on screen).
4. Add the description, the services list with prices, hours and 5–10 real photos (workspace, team at work, screenshots of the demos on a phone). No stock photos.
5. First post: the Lima study, linking to it.
6. Ask every past client for a review by WhatsApp (text ready). No incentives, and ask everyone, not only the happy ones; both are Google policy.
7. Use the same name, phone and website everywhere (Clutch, DesignRush, directories) so Google ties them together, and set the profile link in the site's `LocalBusiness` schema `sameAs` once it exists.
8. The Spanish "Montaweb" collision means our profile needs reviews and the Lima service area to outrank it for "MontaWeb"; the steps above do that.
