## Review: Grok "LatAm horror channel" playbook
Good: honest compliance (labels, consent, no VPN residency trick, 30% US withholding only on US share with W-8BEN), one channel for all countries.
Problems:
- Its own calculator: 100k long-form views/month, 20% US → ~$86 after withholding, below the $92 stack that excludes video generation.
- US RPM $4.77 is an all-content average; Spanish/LatAm long-form is likely ~$0.3–2 RPM (unverified, sources conflict). No source splits US-Hispanic RPM.
- 2 long + 10 shorts/week = 10–20 h/week, above Jan's 5–10.
- Recommends "historia real" keyword while saying never present stories as real; "Archivo del WhatsApp" uses Meta's trademark.
- Missing: Ley 29733 for WhatsApp story intake, SUNAT treatment, stop criteria.

## Review: Grok "AI video and voice for Peruvian businesses" blueprint
Good: real Lima price references for filmed work, correct tax point (advertising is excluded from Nuevo RUS), consent and INDECOPI rules, "don't race to S/ 100 per video".
Problems found with data:
1. **Wrong customer.** Its plan starts by showing samples to "current MontaWeb clients" — there are none, and MontaWeb's buyers pay S/ 50–299 for a website. A S/ 1,758/month (with IGV) retainer is a different customer.
2. **Overpriced vs the real AI competitors.** Kozma 360 (Lima) sells 8 AI reels/month for S/ 1,290 *with* IGV, 4 for S/ 790. Innova Digital: 3 AI videos S/ 300. inteligencia-artificial.com.pe: 3 clips from S/ 249. Grok's S/ 690 for 3 reels is 2–3x the market; Esencial is ~36% above Kozma's 8-reel plan. Low-end community managers charge S/ 500–1,200/month *including* posting.
3. **WhatsApp audios can't be delivered as sold.** Meta marketing templates do not accept audio headers; voice notes go only inside the 24-hour window or to people who saved the number (broadcast lists, max 256). "4 WhatsApp audios a month" has no mass channel.
4. **Voice spots/jingles are weak products.** No verified Peruvian price exists, and human locutores reportedly start at S/ 40–50 (unverified). AI voice has no price advantage there.
5. **Hours.** 12–16 h per Esencial client → 5 clients = 60–80 h/month, i.e. a job, not an extra income source. 30-client projection is impossible solo.
6. **Many of its competitor prices did not verify** (Helios page 404/no prices, Resuelto no prices, Marojhan/Spotman/DevPro/Sala 234 not found).
7. **Spanish copy is not usable as is:** missing accents throughout, and "campana de television… si reemplaza" (bell / "if" instead of campaña / sí) changes meaning. Any es-PE copy must be rewritten under our language rules and read by Jan.
8. Ignores the receptionist already decided here, and the tools Jan already pays for (stack adds HeyGen, Runway, CapCut, Higgsfield ≈ S/ 365/month).

## YouTube: what the data supports
- No verified case of a *new* Spanish AI channel (2023–26) growing to monetization; the big Spanish AI channels with data (Cuentos Facinantes 5.95M subs, Imperio de Jesus 5.87M) were **removed in Jan 2026** in the anti-slop sweep. Channel stats could not be pulled automatically (Social Blade blocks bots) — manual check still needed.
- Enforcement (Jul 2026 clarification): templated videos, AI clips without a story, and AI "experts" on sensitive topics are not monetizable. YouTube says it is tool-agnostic and rewards effort.
- Median RPM by category (AIR, 300 channels, May 2025–May 2026, mostly English audience): education/science $10.22, entertainment $2.43, music $2.28, kids $0.33.
- Consequence: language and niche matter more than tools. 100k views/month ≈ $90 (Spanish horror, Grok's own math) vs ≈ $500–1,000 (English education at $5–10 RPM, before 30% US withholding).

## Proposal A — B2B in Peru (start now)
Principle: sell AI media as **cheap, fixed-price add-ons that help MontaWeb sell sites and the receptionist**, not as an agency retainer.
1. **AI receptionist stays the main AI-voice product** (already decided, S/ 179–349/month, recurring, no monthly production hours). Add a recorded AI greeting / on-hold message to the setup fee.
2. **AI promo reel as the outreach hook.** For each hot census lead, a 15–20 s vertical reel made from their own public Google listing photos, sent privately 1:1 with the site preview. Purpose: get replies. Needs the pending `/lawyer` check on using listing photos in a private preview.
3. **One-off "promo reel" add-on: S/ 149 for 1, S/ 349 for 3 (with IGV)** — at the market level (Innova S/ 150/S/ 300, inteligencia-artificial.com.pe S/ 249 for 3), bundled with a site sale.
4. **Monthly reels pack only after 3 one-off buyers:** 4 reels/month at about S/ 590 with IGV (below Kozma's S/ 790 for 4). Cap at 4 clients until hours are measured.
5. Drop: S/ 1,490 retainer, WhatsApp audio pack, voice spots, jingles, avatar videos with cloned owners (consent overhead for a S/ 50–300 customer).
Tools: Flow/Veo (Gemini Pro) + Picsart/Canva + ElevenLabs. Add only Higgsfield or Kling (~$10–30) if Flow credits run out. Target ≤ 45 min per reel after templates.

## Proposal B — YouTube (a 90-day test, not a business plan yet)
- **One long video per week** (~5–6 h) + 2–3 shorts cut from it. Not 2+10.
- **Recommended niche: English-language documentary narration on Andean/Amazon history and mysteries** (Inca, Nazca, Chavín, Caral, Amazon expeditions). Why: education RPM is the highest measured category, Jan writes English, he lives in Peru (optional own phone footage of sites is a strong "authentic" signal), and it avoids the Spanish RPM problem. Demand for this exact niche is **not yet verified** — first task is a 1-hour manual check of competing channels' views in vidIQ/Social Blade.
- Spanish alternative if Jan prefers Spanish: Peruvian/LatAm legends, but expect Grok's own ~$90 per 100k views.
- Rules: original script per episode, real sources cited in description, synthetic-content label, no fake footage, no batch uploads.
- **Stop rule (day 90, ~12 videos):** continue only if at least one video passed 5,000 views and average view duration is ≥ 35%; otherwise stop or switch niche.
