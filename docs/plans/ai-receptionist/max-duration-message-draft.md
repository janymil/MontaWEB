# Max conversation duration message (es-PE) — draft

Status: draft, waiting for Jan's read-aloud check. Replaces the English default "Conversation ended, goodbye!" (see `settings-2026-11-25.md`).

Spoken when the call reaches the 600 s limit and is cut. Caller: small-business owner on a mobile phone, addressed as usted.

## Options (ranked)

1. Disculpe, se nos acabó el tiempo de la llamada. Uno de nuestros asesores se comunicará con usted.

2. Disculpe, tengo que terminar la llamada. Uno de nuestros asesores se comunicará con usted para seguir conversando.

3. Llegamos al tiempo límite de esta llamada. Uno de nuestros asesores le escribirá por WhatsApp a este número.

## Why this order

- 1 gives the reason in words people use every day ("se nos acabó el tiempo") and does not promise a channel or a time.
- 2 sounds warm, but gives no reason for the cut; some callers may feel they were hung up on.
- 3 is the most concrete, but "tiempo límite" sounds a bit like a system notice, and it commits the team to a WhatsApp follow-up.

## Doubts for Jan

- "se comunicará con usted": standard in Peruvian customer service, a little formal. A warmer variant is "le devuelve la llamada" (the wording the main prompt uses), but only if the team really calls back.
- Option 3 only works if every cut call gets a WhatsApp message from the team.
- No time promise ("pronto", "en breve") on purpose; add one only if the team can keep it.
- "Disculpe" assumes the caller may be cut mid-sentence; if the platform plays the line after silence, it can be dropped.
