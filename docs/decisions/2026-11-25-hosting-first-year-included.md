# Every MontaWeb site includes hosting + SSL for the first year; from year 2 it is S/ 120 a year
Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: decided

## Context
The terms said hosting was free for 6 months, then S/ 120 a year. /servicios said "Año 2: S/ 120/año", which left months 7–12 unclear. Jan asked to fix it "how it should be" and asked what the industry standard is.

## Decision
- Hosting + SSL are included for the **first year** on a montaweb.pe subdomain. From the second year they cost S/ 120 a year, and only if the client renews.
- An own domain stays an optional extra (.com S/ 50, .pe S/ 65 a year). It is not included.
- Updated: servicios, terminos, index, contacto, js/app.js, js/home.js, js/main.js, llms.txt and the /comparar data.

## Why
In Lima the standard is a first year included, then yearly renewal. Bersoft, KOM, Kozma 360 (Web and Tienda), TalentuPeru and Hosting Perú all publish it (quotes in docs/research/2026-11-25-competitor-facts.md and the 2026-11-25 evidence). "6 months" looked worse than the market, and the mismatch between pages was a consumer-information risk (Ley 29571). Hosting a static site costs MontaWeb almost nothing, so a year is cheap to give.
We did not include the domain: at S/ 50 it would wipe out the margin on the S/ 50 landing.

## Consequences / next steps
- Clients signed under the "6 meses" text keep the better of the two: give them the full first year too.
- Re-check /comparar wording if the domain policy changes.
