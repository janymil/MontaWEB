# Six industry landing pages, ordered by hot leads, worded by Peru keywords
Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: decided

## Context
Industry searches in Peru are small (10–90 a month per phrase), but no Lima agency ranks for them, and the census lead score (`2026-11-24-lead-targeting-from-census.md`) tells us which industries pay. The pages double as the link we send in outreach. Research: `docs/research/keywords/2026-11-25-industry-landing-keywords.md`.

## Decision
- Build, in this order: **Veterinarias, Dentistas y clínicas dentales, Clínicas / psicólogos / fisioterapia, Gimnasios, Spas y salones, Hoteles y hostales**. Restaurantes later (most searches, few hot leads). Abogados and inmobiliarias are a redesign pitch, not now.
- Order comes from hot leads; the wording (title, H1, headings, FAQ) comes from the Peru keyword map in the research file.
- Websites start at **S/ 99** (S/ 50 is a different service). Hosting + SSL are included in year 1 (`2026-11-25-hosting-first-year-included.md`).
- The booking feature we sell today is **booking by WhatsApp** (button with a prefilled message). Pages must not promise online booking systems, clinical records, membership software, channel managers or a hotel booking engine. "software / sistema para X" searches are answered honestly in the FAQ (a website + WhatsApp booking, no software needed to start), not claimed as features.
- For vets, mention the AI receptionist add-on only as stated in `2026-11-25-receptionist-number-and-vet-addon.md`.

## Why
Hot leads decide who pays, and keywords decide what they type. No competitor ranks there yet, so even small volumes reach the top 3. Promising features we don't have would cost trust and refunds.

## Consequences / next steps
- One page per industry, each linked to its demo, plus a hub page, sitemap, schema and internal links. es-PE text written by Opus with the Humanizer; Jan reads a sample.
- Bigger generic keywords (diseño de páginas web, tienda virtual, chatbot WhatsApp) are separate pages for later.
- If online booking is built later, update the pages and this decision.
