# AI promo videos get their own page at /videos-publicitarios-con-ia, with links from /servicios, the homepage and the footer
Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: decided (placement, slug, no AI label); copy pending Jan's read

## Context
`2026-11-25-ai-video-voice-income.md` approved AI promo reels at S/ 149 for 1 and S/ 349 for 3 (IGV included). Jan asked for a "premium place" on montaweb.pe. Keyword data: `docs/research/keywords/2026-11-25-ai-video-volume-peru.json` (in branch claude/ai-video-offer-page-c09012).

## Decision
- Primary keyword "videos publicitarios con IA" (70/mo in Peru, buyer intent, only global tools rank). URL `/videos-publicitarios-con-ia`. The DIY terms ("crear videos con ia", 6,600/mo) are not targeted.
- The video offer gets its own page, a new category on /servicios, a compact homepage band below the services section and a footer link. The homepage hero stays on websites.
- The monthly pack shows as "próximamente" with no price, until there are 3 one-off buyers.
- The AI receptionist shows only as "en preparación", with no price, because its decision is still proposed and nothing can be bought yet.
- **No AI label** on the demo reels or the page (Jan decided 2026-11-25: "No label"). The /lawyer check found DS 115-2025-PCM's label duty covers only high-risk AI systems; clients flag AI content with TikTok/Meta's own upload switch. Demo reels still use fictitious businesses, not presented as client work. Prices are shown as final prices with IGV.

## Why
Showing a price for a product nobody can buy, or presenting demo reels as client work, would be misleading advertising (INDECOPI). Websites remain the core product.
