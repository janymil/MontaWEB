# MontaWeb uses Facebook groups as the Semantika TECH Page, and stays off Marketplace
Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: decided

## Context
We wanted visibility in Facebook groups and on Marketplace for MontaWeb's web-design services.

## Decision
- No Marketplace listing. Meta's Commerce Policies ban selling services, except through WhatsApp Business or Appointments.
- Groups are joined and posted to as the Semantika TECH Page (Jan's choice), not Jan's personal profile.
- 2–3 posts a day at most, a different text for every group, following each group's rules. Jan approves every exact text before it goes out.
- The two real-estate agent groups are left out (Jan's call).

## Why
Services on Marketplace risk losing commerce access for the account. Using the Page keeps Jan's personal profile out of it.

## Consequences / next steps
Group list and post log: `docs/research/2026-11-25-facebook-groups.md`. Drafts: `docs/plans/2026-11-25-facebook-posts.md`. For services, use the Page's Services tab and the WhatsApp Business catalogue instead.
