# No blog article on SUNAT and sales via Instagram/WhatsApp (DS 058-2026-EF)
Date: 2026-11-27 · Status: decided

## Context
Pitch: "SUNAT can now inspect sales on Instagram and WhatsApp (DS 058-2026-EF)", to sell websites and shops to social-media sellers. Research: `docs/research/2026-11-27-sunat-ventas-redes-ds-058.md`.

## Decision
No article. Jan confirmed the no-go on 2026-11-27.

## Why
- The decree only lets a SUNAT fedatario buy online as a customer and check the comprobante. It creates no monitoring of social media and no new duty for sellers, so the pitch headline would overstate the law.
- No enforcement case found seven months after it took effect.
- The news peak has passed; problem searches are about zero, and the big tax queries are SUNAT tutorials that don't lead to a website sale.
- A fine depends on issuing a comprobante, not on having a website, so the fit with MontaWeb is weak.

## Consequences / next steps
- Allowed reuse: one accurate FAQ line on /tiendas-virtuales (SUNAT can check online sales by buying as a customer since April 2026; issue a comprobante for every sale), only after reading the official decree text.
- Reconsider if SUNAT publishes results of the undercover purchases, or if the Yape/Plin-as-boleta proposal becomes a norm.
