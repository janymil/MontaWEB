# Blog study: how many Lima business websites show a Libro de Reclamaciones link
Date: 2026-11-27 · Status: decided

## Context
Topic shortlist (`docs/research/2026-11-27-article-topic-shortlist.md`), item 1. Jan chose it on 2026-11-27. The legal facts were re-checked the same day (see the plan).

## Decision
Write an original data study plus supporting pages. The question: how many independent Lima business websites link a virtual Libro de Reclamaciones on the home page? The data comes from a full probe of the Lima web census sites. The study sells MontaWeb websites (every site ships with the link) and LibroDigital (our own virtual libro, already used on montaweb.pe).

## Why
- The legal basis is verified:
  - Res. 0226-2022/SPC-INDECOPI fined an informative site with no online sales: 1.24 UIT for no libro and 0.58 UIT for no aviso.
  - Ley 32495 (11 Nov 2025) adds digital commerce platforms to arts. 150/151, with a permanent visible link.
  - The Reglamento, art. 9, requires the aviso on the home page.
  - The Sala's Yape/BCP ruling (Gestión, 5 Aug 2026) says sites that promote products or services are covered too.
- Our own data is unique: the test probe found that 27% of 290 sites have the link. No one has published such a study.
- Demand: "libro de reclamaciones virtual" 1,900 a month, and the related queries add more. The informative-site angle has thin coverage.

## Limits (must be honest in the text)
- The Yape ruling has no fine, and its number has not been found. The only primary website fine dates from 2022.
- No Indecopi campaign against websites was found. The new reglamento (RM 244-2026-PCM) is still a draft.
- Indecopi offers a free tool ("Tu Libro de Reclamaciones"). The article must mention it.

## Consequences / next steps
- Plan: `docs/plans/2026-11-27-libro-reclamaciones-study-plan.md`. The data is done (29.1% of 2,399 sites, hand-checked), and Jan approved the writing phase on 2026-11-27.
- Jan's answers (2026-11-27): name LibroDigital in the article. No new URL checker: the interactive "check your site" element links to the existing LibroDigital checker, https://librodigital.pe/revisar-mi-web/.
- Final numbers after the image-link spot check (11 of 23 image links were book covers or photos): **688/2,399 = 28.7%**; presentation 18.9%, bookings 26.6%, shops 54.2%; active 33.9% vs 19.3%; Wix 14.8%, Shopify 71.0%; footer 78.5% of links. These replace the 29.1% figures above.
