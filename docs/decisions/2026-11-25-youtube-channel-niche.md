# YouTube channel niche: English documentaries on ancient and pre-Columbian civilizations, with own Peru footage
Date: 2026-11-25 · Status: proposed

## Context
Point 4 of `2026-11-25-ai-video-voice-income.md` (YouTube niche) was reopened because the earlier pick rested on category RPM only. Jan wanted the niche chosen on real growth data, with AI drama evaluated seriously. Constraints: 5–10 h/week, $50–150/month of tools, one channel for all countries, TikTok as traffic only.

## Decision (proposed)
- One English channel: 25–35 min documentaries on ancient and pre-Columbian civilizations of the Americas, with real footage Jan films at Peruvian sites. 1 long video + 2 Shorts a week.
- Stack: Claude, ElevenLabs, Google AI Pro/Flow, Canva/Picsart, free editor. Extra spend $0–34/month.
- 90-day test of 12 videos. Continue if one video reaches 25,000 views or the channel reaches 75,000, with AVD of 30% or more. Stop if the median of videos 7–12 stays under 1,000 views and no video passes 10,000.

## Why
- Browser scan of 19 searches (last ~4 weeks) and 40 channels: new faceless history channels break out now. Ethos (Oct 2026) has 26.6k subs and a median of ~13k views; EL ORIGEN (Spanish, Oct 2026) has 831k/437k/263k.
- Education RPM is the highest category (AIR median $10.22); Spanish LatAm CPM is about 5–15x lower (Peru $0.61, Mexico $0.91, US $14.67).
- AI drama grows fast, but its 45–120 min character stories don't fit 5–10 h, its current medians are low, and it carries high policy risk. Reddit stories and Spanish horror top results are human personalities; no new AI channel broke out.
- Own site footage is the strongest defence against the "inauthentic content" rules in an AI-flooded niche.

## Consequences / next steps
- Biggest risk: channel-level demonetization if the channel looks like mass-produced AI history. Keep the visual and cadence rules in the research file.
- Runner-up if Jan prefers Spanish: same topic in Spanish (more views, much lower RPM, every script needs the full non-English writing and review process).
- Before video 1: check the Flow/Veo commercial terms and the Pro watermark, and which ElevenLabs plan Jan is on.
- If accepted, set point 4 of `2026-11-25-ai-video-voice-income.md` to point here.
- Research: `docs/research/youtube-channel/2026-11-25-niche-recommendation.md` (ranked table, 10 ideas, stack, test), `2026-11-25-channel-data.md`.
